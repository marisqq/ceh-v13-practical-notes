## AI-Assisted Network Scanning

### ShellGPT / ChatGPT for Reconnaissance
- Generate nmap commands from natural language  
- Analyze scan output and suggest next steps  
- Identify potential vulnerabilities from service versions  

### AI in CEH v13 Context
- AI tools can automate scan analysis  
- Parse large nmap XML outputs and summarize findings  
- Correlate discovered services with known CVEs  
- Generate custom scripts for specific targets  

### Exam Note
CEH v13 added AI modules but the practical exam still focuses on traditional tools (nmap, Metasploit, etc). Know that AI can assist but focus on hands-on tool commands.
